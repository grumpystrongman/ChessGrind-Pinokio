How it works

  1. You choose the account. We check that the username exists in Chess.com's public profile directory.
  2. Import your games. Chess Grind fetches publicly accessible games tied to the username you entered—not anyone else's.
  3. Study your decisions. Stockfish reviews the positions and the coach creates individualized learning material.

You can pause future syncing at any time. Existing locally saved games remain in your database and are not deleted when syncing is paused. Online invitations and public sharing remain disabled.
