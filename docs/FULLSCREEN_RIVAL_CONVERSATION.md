ChessGrind Immersive Match and Rival Conversation

All 22 authored rival personas now include distinct conversational openings, turn remarks, praise, mistake reactions, ending remarks, and assigned voice presets/rates. Coaches retain separate fixed identities. Stockfish alone selects chess moves.

Starting a game opens the game board in a full-browser-viewport overlay with a visible speech-bubble conversation rail, rival portrait, move evaluation, and match controls. Users can request browser-native fullscreen. Back to arena preserves the game; Resume current match restores the overlay.

Read only is default. Each rival bubble has Hear this. There is also a Hear last line button, automatic voice toggle, and Stop. Optional local Kokoro neural voice reuses EmberWriter files, with browser speech fallback. Chat remains readable without audio.

Players can type remarks or questions. An explicitly narrow, board-aware persona reply handler covers self-introduction, material count, last move grade, hints, play style, and friendly banter. These are authored exchanges and short computed facts, not open-ended LLM conversations or fabricated engine reasoning. Last 40 bubbles per match persist in browser local storage.

Live assessment remains limited-depth Stockfish; a depth 9 best/mistake/blunder bubble appears after a relevant move and repeated struggle produces board-vision advice. Rivals do not choose engine lines or receive secret access to best moves.
